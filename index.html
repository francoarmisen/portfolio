About

Producer, musician and guitarist.

Based in La Plata, Argentina, I have worked independently since 2015 across music production, recording, mixing, mastering, sound design and guitar arrangements.

I work both remotely and in person, joining projects for a specific stage or following them from the first demo through final delivery.

  • BaseLa Plata · Buenos Aires · Argentina
  • FormatIn person & remote
  • FocusProduction · Audio · Guitars
  • ProjectsSingles · EPs · Albums · Live sessions
